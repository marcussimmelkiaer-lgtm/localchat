# Employee Handbook: Fjordbyte (fictional example company)

This handbook is a made-up example for trying out document search in LocalChat.
Fjordbyte is not a real company. Replace this file with your own documents.

## 1. Working hours and the office

Our core hours are 10:00 to 14:30, Monday to Thursday, and 10:00 to 13:00 on
Fridays. Outside core hours you plan your own day, as long as your team knows
when you can be reached. The office in Aarhus opens at 07:00 and closes at
19:00. Employees who need to work late must tell reception before 17:00 so the
alarm is not set while they are inside.

The office is closed between Christmas Eve and New Year's Day. These days are
paid company holidays and do not count against your vacation balance.

## 2. Remote work

You may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays are "anchor days":
everyone works from the office on Tuesdays unless they are travelling, sick or
on leave. If you want to work fully remotely for a longer period, for example
to stay with family abroad, you can apply for up to six weeks per calendar year.
Applications go to your manager at least one month in advance.

Fjordbyte pays a home-office allowance of 2,500 DKK per year for a chair, desk
or monitor. Keep the receipts and submit them through the expense system.

## 3. Vacation and days off

Every employee gets 27 vacation days per year: the 25 days required by Danish
law plus two extra "Fjordbyte days". Vacation days are earned monthly and can
be used from the month after they are earned. You can carry at most five unused
days over into the next vacation year; the rest are paid out.

Your birthday is a day off with pay. If your birthday falls on a weekend, you
can take the Friday before or the Monday after.

Requests for more than five consecutive vacation days must be made at least
four weeks in advance, so teams can plan around them. Summer vacation requests
(June to August) must be submitted before 1 March.

## 4. Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 09:00 on the first day, by phone or
text message, not only by email. You do not need a doctor's note for the first
three days. From the fourth sick day in a row, we need a note from your doctor.

If your child is sick, you may take the child's first and second sick day off
with full pay. Let your manager know in the same way as for your own sickness.

## 5. Parental leave

Fjordbyte pays full salary for 24 weeks of parental leave, for every parent,
regardless of gender. Leave can be split into several periods before the
child's third birthday. Tell your manager about planned leave at least three
months before it starts. You keep your pension contributions during the leave.

## 6. Expenses and travel

Business expenses are reimbursed through the expense system within 30 days of
the purchase. Upload a photo of every receipt. Purchases over 5,000 DKK need
approval from your manager before you buy.

When travelling for work, meals are covered up to 450 DKK per day within
Denmark and 600 DKK per day abroad. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client
dinners approved in advance.

Travel by train when the journey takes less than four hours. Flights are only
booked for longer journeys, and always in economy class. Hotels should cost at
most 1,400 DKK per night in Denmark; abroad, use the company's booking portal,
which shows the approved limit for each city.

## 7. Equipment

Each employee gets a laptop, a monitor, a headset and a phone. Laptops are
replaced every three years, or sooner if they break. You may choose between a
Windows laptop and a MacBook. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT
within 24 hours.

You may use your work laptop for reasonable private use, such as online
banking, but not for running your own business or for storing large private
photo collections.

## 8. IT security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password
manager instead of writing passwords down. Two-factor authentication is
required for email, the expense system and all customer data.

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links. Forward it to
security@fjordbyte.example and delete it. Reporting a phishing email you
clicked by mistake is never punished: the faster IT knows, the easier it is to
fix.

Customer data must never be stored on USB sticks or personal cloud accounts.
Screens must be locked whenever you leave your desk (Windows + L, or
Control + Command + Q on a Mac).

## 9. Learning and development

Every employee has a learning budget of 12,000 DKK per year and five learning
days for courses, conferences or certifications. Unused budget does not carry
over. Talk to your manager about how the course fits your role before you
book it.

On the last Friday of every month, the afternoon is reserved for "Fjord
Fridays": internal talks where colleagues share something they have learned.

## 10. Who to contact

- Questions about salary, pension or leave: people@fjordbyte.example
- IT problems or lost equipment: it@fjordbyte.example, or call extension 400
- Security incidents: security@fjordbyte.example
- Everything else: ask your manager, or the reception desk on the ground floor
